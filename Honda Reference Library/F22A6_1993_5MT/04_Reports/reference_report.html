Falta verificar

  • KA/KL and exact emissions certification are unknown. Preserve both catalogues; ECU and catalyst differ. A catalogue selection cannot determine the user’s car.
  • Exact F22A6 cam lobe count, axial spacing, holder count, lobe width, lift law, opening/closing events and overlap were not established by a readable applicable primary source.16 valves / 16 rockers do not independently prove 16 lobes.
  • External dimensions, bore pitch, rod centre distance, pin offset, chamber geometry, wall thickness, oil-gallery routing and fastener spacing need dimensioned drawings or physical measurement. No scale can be inferred from shipping dimensions or uncalibrated photos.
  • OEM catalogue proves bypass body, diaphragm, vacuum tank, solenoid/check valve. Exact activation threshold, hysteresis, full vacuum routing and calibrated control logic remain unverified for the target.
  • A complete verified1993 US F22A6 workshop manual 61SM405 was not acquired. Do not label1996 F22B1 tolerances, valve clearances, tightening torques or timing settings as F22A6.
  • No set of six directional full-engine photographs with visible F22A6 stamp was verified. One1993 EX dealer engine-bay image supports shared exterior appearance only;60 exact OEM component service photos are available.
  • 140 hp is verified in Honda’s1993 EX sedan brochure.5600 rpm / 142 lb-ft @ 4500 rpm are familiar secondary values but not promoted to verified here because the available primary table is too small to read confidently.
  • OEM diagram silhouettes and dealer service photographs do not establish alloy grades, internal surface finishes or production tolerances.
