HONDA ACCORD MOTOR PROJECT — REFERENCE LIBRARY

Open index.html for the offline diagram library.

To feed the teardown skill:
1. Supply 01_Research/Honda_F22_Engine_Skill_Brief.md (or the .txt copy).
2. Supply the ten original photos in ../Pictures of engine/.
3. Supply the relevant diagrams in 02_OEM_Diagrams/ and their same-name JSON
   parts tables. Catalog_Parts.csv combines all 489 catalog entries.
4. Use the service supplement and cited factory manual for measurements and
   operation. The exploded drawings are not dimensioned manufacturing plans.

CONTENTS
- 20 full-size OEM diagram PNGs.
- 20 matching JSON parts tables, with callouts and source URLs.
- Research brief in Markdown and plain text.
- 280-page 1996 Honda Accord Coupe/Aerodeck service supplement PDF.
- Combined catalog CSV, source reading list, download manifest and checksums.

The photographed engine is consistent with SOHC VTEC, but its stamped code
is still unconfirmed. F22B1 / 1994 US Accord EX is a provisional reference.
The KL 4AT catalog does not establish your donor engine's market or gearbox.
The 1996 supplement is NOT the complete 1994–1997 shop manual.

CATALOG NOTES
Require Quantity means installed requirement; Package Quantity is a sales
quantity. Alternative sizes, bearing grades, suppliers and replacement part
numbers must not be modeled as additional simultaneously installed parts.
Original photos remain in your existing Pictures of engine folder.
